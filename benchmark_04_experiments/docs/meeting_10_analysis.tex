\begin{abstract}
This report preserves the Algorithm 4 and Algorithm 5 studies and evaluates
Algorithm 6 as the current evidence-gated successor. The evidence base contains
a 53-system comparison plus Algorithm 6, 464 collision-free unique OCR
query-target pairs, and 66,257 unique synthetic clean-core pairs, all ranked
against the same 17,476-family Egyptian medicine catalog. Algorithm 6 preserves
Algorithm 5's 50.4310\% OCR Hit@1 and raises Hit@20 from 72.8448\% to
73.2759\% by recovering two additional candidates with zero paired losses. It
preserves 98.1889\% Hit@1 and 99.9985\% Hit@20 on synthetic data. A proposed
rank-1 consensus gate is rejected because its 27 synthetic gains accompany 138
losses. Automated label audit assigns 138 OCR pairs to P0, 56 to P1, and 270
to provisional P2; source-image references are absent, so the historical
93-pair holdout remains retrospective rather than fresh blind evidence. A
cross-fitted correctness gate accepts 99 of 211 P2 development pairs and 27 of
59 P2 retrospective holdout pairs with no observed error, but every accepted
result still requires user confirmation. The pharmacist study has 1,125
balanced Algorithm 6 assignments and no participant outcomes.
\end{abstract}

\section{Key Findings}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Algorithm 6 is the current system at 50.4310\% Hit@1 and
        73.2759\% Hit@20 on 464 OCR pairs.} It preserves every Algorithm 5
        rank-1 outcome and adds two top-20 recoveries with zero paired losses.
  \item \textbf{The broad rank-1 consensus rule was not deployed.} It gains
        three and loses zero OCR rank-1 cases, but gains 27 and loses 138
        synthetic rank-1 cases; the apparent OCR gain does not transfer.
  \item \textbf{Algorithm 5 remains the strongest base ranker in the
        53-system comparison.} Its 50.4310\% Hit@1 and 72.8448\% Hit@20 improve
        frozen Algorithm 4 by 2.8017 and 1.7241 percentage points on the same
        464 pairs.
  \item \textbf{No external method closes the observed-OCR gap.} The best
        external Hit@1 is 38.5776\%, while the best external Hit@20 is
        67.4569\%; paired case switches and Holm-adjusted McNemar tests identify
        whether each gap is consistent across queries.
  \item \textbf{Synthetic ranking does not transfer directly to OCR.}
        Algorithm 5 reaches 98.1889\% Hit@1 and 99.9985\% Hit@20 on 66,257
        clean-core pairs, while edit-distance systems approach that synthetic
        ceiling but remain materially weaker on the OCR pairs.
  \item \textbf{The current source differs from the historical clean-core
        artifact.} Forty-four pairs gain rank 1, 55 lose rank 1, and 187 ranks
        change; the current result is lower by 11 Hit@1 cases and has one
        top-20 miss, \code{aclox}$\rightarrow$\code{ADOX}.
  \item \textbf{Candidate rescue, not one tie rule, supplies the main gain.}
        Removing Algorithm 5 family rescue lowers OCR Hit@1 by 13.7931 points
        and Hit@20 by 20.0431 points; removing all reranking has a much larger
        Hit@1 effect than Hit@20 effect.
  \item \textbf{The remaining failures separate into demonstrated and
        unsupported fixes.} Algorithm 6 reduces Algorithm 5's 126 OCR top-20
        misses to 124, but 116 remaining misses are P0 label/evidence conflicts
        and eight are P1; no P2 row is a top-20 miss under the audit rule.
  \item \textbf{Label reliability limits the next accuracy claim.} No source
        image is linked to any of the 464 OCR pairs, and both new top-20
        recoveries are P0/P1 cases. Candidate recovery is measured; target
        validity is not yet adjudicated.
  \item \textbf{The 0.60 extreme cohort remains a low-evidence stress test.}
        It contains 113 fair OCR pairs after deduplication and collision
        removal; complete per-system results, operation distributions, and
        concrete rows are reported rather than discarded.
  \item \textbf{Human-study claims are not available.} The counterbalanced
        protocol contains 15 participants, 75 crops, and 1,125 assignments,
        but accuracy, decision time, and safety cannot be reported until real
        responses and adjudicated labels exist.
\end{itemize}

Compact form lowercases text and removes non-alphanumeric characters. For
\code{CLEX A NE} $\rightarrow$ \code{CLEXANE}, source alignment counts two
spaces as edits, while both compact strings are \code{clexane} and compact
distance is zero. With compact query $q_c$, target $y_c$, and Levenshtein
distance $L$,
\[
d=L(q_c,y_c), \qquad r=\frac{d}{\max(|y_c|,1)}.
\]
An extreme prediction has $r>0.60$. For \code{GHONT} $\rightarrow$
\code{CALAMINE}, $d=7$, $|y_c|=8$, and $r=0.875$. The 0.60 threshold is an
operational review band, not a probability or clinical-risk estimate.

\subsection{Metrics and Statistical Tests}

For query $q$, $R_k(q)$ is the first $k$ returned families and $Y(q)$ is the
verified relevant-family set:
\[
\operatorname{Hit@k}(q)=\mathbf{1}\!\left[R_k(q)\cap Y(q)\neq\emptyset\right].
\]

\begin{itemize}
  \item Hit@1 failure with Hit@20 success is a ranking failure; Hit@20 failure
        is a candidate-retrieval failure.
  \item MRR@20 uses reciprocal rank for the first relevant result through rank
        20 and zero beyond rank 20.
  \item Paired risk differences use 10,000 bootstrap resamples with seed
        20260719. Error bars are 95\% percentile intervals.
  \item Exact two-sided McNemar tests use pairwise gains and losses. Matched odds
        ratios describe effect direction. Exploratory p-values are not adjusted
        for multiple comparisons and are not treated as confirmatory evidence.
  \item Spearman correlation measures monotonic association. It does not assign
        causality to edit distance, evidence counts, or recovery.
\end{itemize}

\section{Equal Edit-Distance Ordering}

Equal raw edit distance leaves 37 primary ranking failures unresolved, so eight
generic evidence policies were tested without privileging a specific character
position.

A generic rule is allowed to act only when the query is brand-like, the current
first candidate has nonzero raw distance, another candidate has the same raw
distance, and its full-model score is within the configured gap. Lower weighted
distance is better; higher position, edge, agreement, and full score are better.

\subsection{Learned OCR Confusion Evidence}

Directional costs are learned only from the 211 provisional P2 development
rows. For substitution count $c(o,t)$ and the largest count for observed
character $o$, $m(o)$, the cost is
\[
w(o,t)=\max\left(0.20,\,
1-0.80\frac{c(o,t)}{m(o)}\frac{c(o,t)}{c(o,t)+3}\right).
\]
Missing- and extra-character costs use the same frequency shrinkage with scale
0.55 and floor 0.45. Unseen operations cost 1. A lower cost means the operation
appeared more consistently in eligible OCR alignments; it does not mean the

\section{Failures and Dead Ends}

These findings identify work that failed, remains unsupported, or would produce
misleading claims if reported as success.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The remaining OCR misses are not clean optimization targets.}
        Algorithm 6 has 124 top-20 misses; 116 are P0 and eight are P1.
        (No P2 row is missed, but P2 selection itself uses candidate-union
        presence, so this is an audit result rather than unbiased accuracy.)
  \item \textbf{Both Algorithm 6 gains still need crop review.}
        \code{LACTULOSE}$\rightarrow$\code{LACTO} is P1 and
        \code{TEFA}$\rightarrow$\code{TELFAST} is P0. (The system demonstrably
        retrieves the supplied target, but the absent images prevent a claim
        that the supplied target is valid.)
  \item \textbf{The learned ranker does not beat deterministic Algorithm 6.}
        Its cross-fit development Hit@20 is 68.7332\% versus 71.6981\%, and its
        retrospective holdout Hit@20 is 77.4194\% versus 79.5699\%.
        (It remains an analysis model, not a deployed override.)
  \item \textbf{The rank-1 consensus gate fails transfer.} It gains 27 and
        loses 138 synthetic rank-1 cases. (Three zero-loss OCR gains are not
        enough to justify a rule that regresses the larger locked set.)
  \item \textbf{Synthetic accuracy overstates observed-OCR transfer.}
        Edit-distance variants exceed 94\% Hit@1 on generated clean-core edits
        but remain below 39\% on the fair OCR pairs. (Controlled mutation rules
        do not reproduce the complete observed corruption distribution.)
  \item \textbf{Autocorrect artifacts contain insufficient signal.} Algorithm 4
        reaches 0\% Hit@1 and 3.65\% Hit@20. (Mappings such as a medicine to an
        unrelated English word require image evidence, a language model with
        constraints, or human clarification, not ordinary fuzzy matching.)
  \item \textbf{Keyboard-shift results are feature-specific.} Algorithm 4 reaches
        0.8\% Hit@1 and 2.0\% Hit@20. (Algorithms 1 and 3 score much higher
        because they contain a whole-keyboard shift reversal; the category tests
        that decoder, not general spelling robustness.)
  \item \textbf{Generic equal-distance rules did not transfer.} Edge-first
        improved development by 0.54 points but reduced holdout by 3.23 points.
        (No policy replaced current ordering.)
  \item \textbf{Most atomic switches are unproven on 464 pairs.} Only four of
        54 atomic Algorithm 5 flags change Hit@1 or Hit@20 on the locked OCR
        denominator. (Zero top-k delta can still hide lower-rank or safety
        changes, so these switches require larger targeted tests before removal.)
  \item \textbf{Raw OCR-model ranking is confounded.} Models did not process a
        matched image set. (Case severity, target distribution, and row count
        differ by model.)
  \item \textbf{DrugEye has no matched human-study outcome.} Historical web
        probes and cached sample results do not replace randomized participant
        decisions. (No clinical or workflow superiority claim is made.)
  \item \textbf{Training dynamics are not defined for this run.} The study
        evaluates frozen string scorers, indexes, and published dense
        checkpoints without fine-tuning. (There are no epochs, losses,
        gradients, learning-rate schedules, seeds, or early-stopping events to
        plot.)
  \item \textbf{The selective gate is not clinical calibration.} It models
        top-1 correctness under historical supplied labels and reaches zero
        observed errors only on provisional P2 rows. (Its output remains
        ``likely match, confirmation required''; probability calibration across
        all systems or clinical outcomes is still unsupported.)
  \item \textbf{The static browser is not the full Python Algorithm 6.}
        GitHub Pages cannot execute its consensus indexes. (The browser retains
        Algorithm 5 ordering with Algorithm 6 safety states until a backend or
        exported equivalent is deployed.)
  \item \textbf{GPU-hour and peak-memory efficiency were not logged.} The
        reproducible efficiency fields are one-time preparation time and
        warm-query latency on the recorded machine. (A deployment benchmark
