# Strict product-selection failure history

## Run 20260822T171120Z - five oracle failures

The first 440-row execution passed 435 rows and failed five. The original
per-case outcomes and summary remain versioned under
`runs/20260822T171120Z/`; the content-addressed raw-response log stays in the
local evaluation archive and can be regenerated by the evaluator.

| Cases | Symptom | Root cause | Classification | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LEVOXIN 500 MG | A vial ID was returned in addition to the tablet ID. | The generator ignored a sibling whose catalog `st` contained `500MG; 100ML`. | Label/oracle defect | Exclude families containing composite sibling metadata from the simple-strength benchmark. |
| CAZET 10 MG; LIPITRIN 10 MG | Combination products were returned as compatible. | The catalog `st` field stored only one component while the commercial name contained `10/40MG` or similar. | Label/oracle defect | Exclude families with printed multi-strength combinations from this simple-strength benchmark. |
| EXAMIDE 10 MG | Only the tablet was selected instead of also treating the injection as an equal tie. | The injection was printed as `10MG/ML` while its `st` field contained only `10MG`; the generator incorrectly treated the records as identical evidence. | Label/oracle defect | Exclude families whose printed concentration denominator is absent from `st`. |
| CHOLEROSE 40 MG | The plain 40 MG product was selected without the 10/40 MG combination. | The generator treated the single recorded `40MG` field as the entire combination signature. | Label/oracle defect | Apply the same printed-combination exclusion. |

No runtime algorithm change was justified.  The independent oracle was made
more conservative, the dataset was regenerated from the same frozen catalog,
and the replacement rows were selected by the same fixed seed.
